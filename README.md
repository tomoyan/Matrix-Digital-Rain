# Matrix Digital Rain

A full-screen interactive visualizer inspired by the iconic green "digital rain" code from *The Matrix* (1999).

The app renders falling streams of mirrored Japanese katakana, numbers, and symbols on a pure black screen. Leading characters glow bright white-green while their trails slowly fade and mutate in real time.

---

## How It Works

- **Full-Screen Rain**: Covers the entire page with smooth, high-DPI falling code streams.
- **Interactive Ripples**: Move your mouse or drag your finger across the screen to light up nearby characters. Click or tap anywhere to launch a burst of fresh streams.
- **Clean View**: Only two small buttons sit in the bottom-right corner (Audio and Settings). They dim automatically when you stop moving the mouse, and disappear completely in Screensaver Mode.

---

## Settings & Features

Click the **Settings** button in the bottom-right corner (or press `C`) to open the control panel. Click or tap anywhere outside the panel to close it.

### Quick Actions
- **Pause / Resume**: Freeze the falling code in place or start it again.
- **Fullscreen**: Expand the app to fill your entire monitor.
- **Reset**: Restore all settings back to their original defaults.

### Ambient Sound
- **Mute / Unmute Switch**: Turns on a procedural background sound layer featuring a warm CRT monitor hum and rhythmic digital clicking sounds.
- **Vertical Sound Volume Bar**: Hovering over the unmuted audio icon displays a vertical volume bar (`5%`–`100%`) so you can drag up or down to adjust the level. Moving your pointer away automatically hides the volume bar and returns the audio icon to its default color.

### Rain Color
- **Preset Themes**: Switch between **Classic Green**, **Amber**, **Blue**, **Red**, **Teal**, and **Violet**.
- **Random Cycle**: Gradually blends the rain through random colors over time. When selected, a **Color Shift Pace** slider (`2s`–`15s`) lets you control how fast the colors transition.
- **Custom Color Picker**: Pick any color from the color swatch or type a `#RRGGBB` hex code.

### Rain Sliders
- **Fall Speed** (`0.25×`–`2.50×`): Controls how fast the code streams fall down the screen.
- **Stream Density** (`35%`–`100%`): Controls how many columns of code are falling at once.
- **Glyph Size** (`12px`–`28px`): Changes the size of the characters.
- **Trail Length** (`10`–`44 cells`): Controls how long the glowing tail stays visible behind each falling stream.
- **Phosphor Glow** (`0%`–`100%`): Adjusts the bright halo glow around the leading characters.

### Visual & Behavior Toggles
- **Mirrored Katakana**: Flips the characters horizontally like the original movie title sequence.
- **Background Depth Layer**: Adds a second, smaller, slower layer of rain in the background for a 3D depth effect.
- **CRT Scanlines**: Adds subtle horizontal monitor lines and a soft dark edge vignette.
- **Pointer Ripple**: Enables or disables mouse and touch ripple effects on the rain.
- **Auto Screensaver**: Automatically hides all buttons, hides the cursor, and slows the rain to a calm, hypnotic pace after a period of inactivity. Includes an **Inactivity Delay** slider (`10s`–`120s`) and a **Start Screensaver Now** button. Move the mouse, tap, or press any key to wake the screen.

## Keyboard Shortcuts

- **`Space`** — Pause or resume the rain
- **`M`** — Mute or unmute ambient sound
- **`C`** — Open or close the settings panel
- **`F`** — Toggle fullscreen mode
- **`H`** — Hide or show all on-screen controls
- **`Esc`** — Close the settings panel or restore hidden controls
